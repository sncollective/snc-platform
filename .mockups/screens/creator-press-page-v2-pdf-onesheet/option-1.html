About

Punk-leaning rock that hits where it hurts.

Raw, funny, and unpredictable, the Fort Collins band confronts mental health, addiction, and life under a corporate-run world. LeAnna Warren swings from Paramore-power belting to unapologetic screams, carrying sharp humor into songs built to hit where it hurts.

Fueled by the fury of the forgotten, Animal Future veers from indie rock and grungy pop to old-school punk, with Radiohead-esque soundscapes keeping every turn unstable. Their electric live energy creates a space for connection — an urgent rallying cry for individuality, compassion, people, and the planet.

For fans of

IDLES · Radiohead · Modest Mouse · Pixies · Yeah Yeah Yeahs · Paramore
