About the band

Punk-leaning rock that hits where it hurts.

Raw, funny, and unpredictable, Animal Future confronts mental health, addiction, and life under a corporate-run world. LeAnna Warren’s voice moves from Paramore-power belting to unapologetic screams while the band splices indie rock, grungy pop, old-school punk, and Radiohead-esque atmosphere. Their live energy makes the tension communal.
