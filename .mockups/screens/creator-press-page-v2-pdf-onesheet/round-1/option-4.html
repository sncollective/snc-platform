About

Animal Future confronts mental health, addiction, and life under a corporate-run world. LeAnna Warren’s voice swings from Paramore-power belting to unapologetic screams while the band crosses indie rock, grungy pop, old-school punk, and Radiohead-esque atmosphere. Sharp humor turns that tension into connection.
