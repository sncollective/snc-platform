Animal Future

Fort Collins, COSocially conscious punk / alt-rock
About

Animal Future makes socially conscious punk-leaning rock that hits where it hurts.

Raw, funny, and unpredictable, the Fort Collins band confronts mental health, addiction, and life under a corporate-run world, fronted by a female vocalist who swings from Paramore-power belting to unapologetic screams.

Fueled by the fury of the forgotten, Animal Future moves from indie rock to grungy pop and old-school punk, with Radiohead-esque soundscapes adding unpredictability.

Known for sharp humor and electric energy, they create a space for connection that turns every show into a rallying cry for individuality and compassion — for people and the planet.
